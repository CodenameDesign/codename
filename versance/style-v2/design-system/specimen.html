Compliance finding

Overly Promotional Language and Unbalanced Disclosure

High risk
NP 51-201, Section 2.1(2); CSA Staff Notice 51-356

The news release uses promotional and potentially unbalanced language, particularly in describing the South Railroad project as a 'significant permitting milestone' that 'unlocks next North American growth project,' and in statements such as 'Equinox Gold is… positioned as the new North American senior gold producer.' This may create an impression of certainty and success that is not fully supported by the underlying facts.

Suggested refinement: Replace promotional phrases with factual, neutral language. Avoid statements that imply certainty about future production or status as a 'senior gold producer' unless supported by objective metrics. Ensure forward-looking statements are clearly identified and balanced with risks.

How the Versance Coworker Works

How the Versance Coworker Works

1. Choose a primary company

Select a company at the top. All of its filings and news are added to context, so you can ask me anything about it. Switch to another company at any time.

2. Add documents to research context

Open the CAD or US library, find any company, and add the filings or news releases you want to compare to your research context. You can also upload your own documents or add a web page.

3. Ask and compare

Ask me questions across your primary company's record, the documents in your research context and the page you're working on. Every answer shows its sources.

4. Run a compliance review

Open Compliance review to check the page you have open, whether it's your own draft or a document you're reviewing, against Canadian or US securities regulations.
