New Markets, New Routes

Canada could add $146 billion in non-U.S. export growth by 2035, with the right investment in transportation, logistics, and processing capacity, according to a new PwC Canada report. It evaluated Canada's export potential against five criteria measuring how viable non-U.S. markets are for each product group, given the right logistics infrastructure.
